# Code of conduct

## Our standard

MADGER welcomes careful, constructive participation. Be respectful, factual, patient with
newcomers, and attentive to security and privacy. Debate ideas without attacking people. Disclose
project affiliation when relevant and never manufacture endorsement, urgency, or financial hype.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, impersonation,
credential or wallet solicitation, spam, deceptive promotion, market manipulation, publishing
private information, or knowingly spreading false project, token, listing, partnership, or trading
claims.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository activity and official MADGER community spaces when someone is
representing the project. Platform-specific rules remain binding.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `madgercoin@gmail.com`. Do not place sensitive evidence in a
public issue. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or escalate serious safety
concerns. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate,
and protect reporters from retaliation.
