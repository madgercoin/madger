UTILITY OPERATING LOOP

Listen. Build. Prove. Improve.

Listen for friction

Collect specific community problems: onboarding confusion, impersonation risk, missing information, creator uncertainty, and weak communication.

Ship a useful response

Release a guide, source, mission, brief, correction, or working standard that directly addresses the problem.

Keep the evidence public

Date the release, link the source, credit accepted contributions, and improve the next cycle without rewriting history.

The Commons is the first operating utility. Use the live utility hub, then inspect contributor terms in the public framework.

PROPOSED EXPANSION REVIEW

Build carefully. Keep the likeness exact.

Value & feasibility
What does this add to The Burrow, and can it be delivered with available resources?
Security & compliance
What could expose people, assets, or the project to harm? What review is required?
Accessibility & operations
Can people use it, and who will maintain, support, and communicate it?
Identity & permissions
Does every visual preserve the approved likeness, and are the rights clearly documented?

One MADGER. No substitutes. Preserve the approved face, markings, expression, proportions, and signature fang. New visual concepts must meet the same identity standard before release.
