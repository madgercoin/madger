Direction, not a promise.

The stages describe an operating sequence, not a claim that every milestone is complete. Evidence criteria are proposed execution guidance. Timing and execution depend on approvals, resources, security, and feasibility. See the living launch record for current verified facts and open items.

THE OPERATING SEQUENCE

Four stages. Evidence at every step.

  1. 01

    STAGE 01

    Foundation

    Establish the brand system, website, verifiable token record, core documents, and recognized communication channels.

    Evidence to look for

    Approved assets and accessible, consistent public sources.

  2. 02

    STAGE 02

    Readiness

  3. 03

    STAGE 03

    Distribution

    Build repeatable publishing, education, collaboration, and community-operations programs.

    Evidence to look for

    STAGE 04

    Expansion

    Evaluate merchandise, media, games, collectibles, licensing, and payments through small, controlled tests.

DISTRIBUTION

Turn the character into culture.

Publish & educate

Original stories, cinematic work, investor briefings, project dispatches, and clear explanations of official information and verification practices.

Collaborate & support

Defined contributor scopes, recognized channels, responsible moderation, and practical protection against impersonation.

Learn & refine

Review released work and community feedback. Use that learning to improve the next cycle without diluting the identity.

Bring value. Keep receipts. Document scope, permissions, compensation, incentives, and expectations before substantial commissioned work begins. Read the contributor framework.

PROPOSED EXPANSION REVIEW

Build carefully. Keep the likeness exact.

Value & feasibility
What does this add to The Burrow, and can it be delivered with available resources?
Security & compliance
What could expose people, assets, or the project to harm? What review is required?
Accessibility & operations
Can people use it, and who will maintain, support, and communicate it?
Identity & permissions
Does every visual preserve the approved likeness, and are the rights clearly documented?

One MADGER. No substitutes. Preserve the approved face, markings, expression, proportions, and signature fang. New visual concepts must meet the same identity standard before release.
