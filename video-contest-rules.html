01 / CONTEST PERIOD

ONE WINDOW. NO LATE ENTRIES.

Opening

The contest opens September 9, 2026 at 12:00 AM Eastern Time.

Deadline

The contest closes September 22, 2026 at 11:59 PM Eastern Time. A submission must be complete by that time to qualify.

02 / ELIGIBILITY

WHO MAY ENTER.

Age and jurisdiction

  • Entrants must be 18 years of age or older.
  • Participation and receipt of a $MADGER prize must be lawful where the entrant lives.
  • Entrants must be legally able to receive and hold $MADGER on Solana.

Multiple entries

An entrant may submit multiple genuinely distinct qualifying videos. Duplicate or substantially duplicated work may not occupy multiple Top 10 prize positions. MADGER team members directly involved in judging are not eligible to win.

03 / HOW TO ENTER

EVERY REQUIREMENT MUST BE COMPLETE.

Create and post

  1. Create an original MADGER video using the approved current contest resources.
  2. Use the required official intro at the beginning and official outro at the end.
  3. Post the video publicly on any eligible public platform.
  4. Include the exact hashtag #MadgerMeme.
  5. Keep the post publicly viewable through winner verification.

Submit the original

  1. Complete the official submission form.
  2. Provide the direct public-post URL.
  3. Upload the highest-quality original exported video file.
  4. Name the file exactly after the video title, followed only by its file extension.
  5. Provide a valid Solana wallet address and all required attestations.
Conforming entry rule: a creative video is an entry only when every mandatory requirement is satisfied. A correctable issue may be fixed and resubmitted before the deadline; after the deadline, incomplete or nonconforming entries do not qualify.

06 / CONTENT STANDARDS

MAKE IT BOLD. KEEP IT USABLE.

Allowed creative tools

AI-assisted creation, animation, voiceover, music, sound design and VFX are allowed. The entrant remains responsible for likeness compliance, originality, rights clearance and final quality.

Prohibited content

  • Hate speech or discriminatory content.
  • Pornography or sexually explicit material.
  • Threats, harassment, illegal activity or targeted abuse.
  • Scams, phishing, wallet-draining links or deceptive financial claims.
  • Stolen or unauthorized footage, music, artwork, voices or other third-party material.
  • Claims that MADGER guarantees profits, returns or investment performance.

07 / JUDGING

LIKENESS FIRST. QUALITY AFTER.

MADGER likeness and mandatory-rule compliance are pass/fail gates. Only conforming entries proceed to ranking.

Ranking criteria

  • Creativity and originality
  • Entertainment value and watchability
  • Quality of execution
  • Storytelling or concept strength
  • Brand fit
  • Shareability and viral potential
  • Overall impact

Tie-break order

  1. Stronger overall creative concept
  2. Better execution
  3. Greater shareability or audience response
  4. Final good-faith determination by the MADGER team

Engagement may inform judging but does not automatically determine the winner.
