# MADGER App Privacy Summary

Effective: August 28, 2026  
Last updated: September 9, 2026

The MADGER app works without an account. It does not connect to a cryptocurrency wallet, request a seed phrase or private key, execute token transactions, or custody assets.

The Daily Dig stores its date and streak count locally on the device. That information is not transmitted by the app. The app contains no advertising identifier or third-party advertising SDK.

The app's purchase buttons open `madgercoin.com` fixed redirect routes before sending the user to Raydium, Jupiter, BONKbot, or Trojan. MADGER's website may record aggregate campaign source and route-click counters through Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine. The MADGER acquisition dataset is designed without cookies, wallet addresses, names, email addresses, IP-address fields, advertising identifiers, browser fingerprints, or persistent user identifiers. These counters are used to compare aggregate outreach and route activity; they do not identify a purchaser or prove that a particular click caused an on-chain purchase.

External destinations, public blockchains, the device operating system, and network providers handle activity under their own privacy terms and practices.
