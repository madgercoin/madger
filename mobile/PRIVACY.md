# MADGER App Privacy Summary

Effective: August 28, 2026

The MADGER app works without an account. It does not connect to a cryptocurrency wallet, request a seed phrase or private key, execute token transactions, or custody assets.

The Daily Dig stores its date and streak count locally on the device. That information is not transmitted by the app. This release contains no advertising or third-party analytics.

When a user opens madgercoin.com, Telegram, X, email, or native sharing, that destination handles the action under its own privacy terms.

Questions: madgercoin@gmail.com

Before store submission, publish the approved policy at `https://madgercoin.com/privacy.html` and ensure it matches the shipped app.
