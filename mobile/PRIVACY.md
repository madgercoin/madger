# MADGER App Privacy Summary

Effective: August 28, 2026  
Last updated: October 30, 2026

The MADGER app works without an account. It does not connect to a cryptocurrency wallet, request a seed phrase or private key, execute token transactions, or custody assets.

Burrow Run stores high scores, the last 20 runs and their statistics, collected signals, daily field marks, play dates, XP, achievements, earned trails, and sound/haptic/calm preferences locally on the device. Expedition coins, relics, upgrades, hero unlocks, discoveries and unfinished-run checkpoints also remain local. Existing Daily Dig date and streak data remains local. This information is not transmitted by the app. Players can reset game progress from Settings, or remove all local app data by clearing storage or uninstalling. Sharing a best score opens the operating system share sheet and sends it only to a destination the player chooses. The app contains no advertising identifier or third-party advertising SDK.

The app contains no token-purchase buttons, trading guides, exchange links, or wallet tools. Optional support, privacy, website and community links open only when selected. External websites and community services have their own privacy practices; the app does not send saved game records to those destinations.

Optional game sound effects are bundled with the app and play on the device. The app does not request microphone or audio-recording access, and game audio stops when paused or backgrounded.

External destinations, public blockchains, the device operating system, and network providers handle activity under their own privacy terms and practices.
