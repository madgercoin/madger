Privacy
Policy

Effective August 28, 2026 · Last updated August 28, 2026

Plain-language summary: The MADGER app works without an account, wallet connection, advertising, or third-party analytics. The Daily Signal streak remains on your device.

Information the app stores

The Daily Signal feature saves only the last check-in date and streak count in the app’s local device storage. This information is used to display the streak, is not sent to MADGER, and can be removed by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Information the app does not collect

  • No name, account profile, phone number, or location is required.
  • No advertising identifier or third-party analytics SDK is included in this release.
  • No cryptocurrency wallet is connected or monitored.
  • No seed phrase, private key, wallet credential, or payment information is requested.
  • The app does not execute transactions, sell tokens, or custody assets.

External services

Links to madgercoin.com, Telegram, X, email, and native sharing open only after the user selects them. Those destinations and the device operating system process activity under their own privacy terms. MADGER does not control their independent practices.

Launch Hunt entries

The app prepares a user-initiated email to enter the Launch Hunt. The user chooses whether to send it. Information placed in that email is processed to administer the promotion, verify eligibility and entries, contact winners, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. Entry details are not sold.

Children

The app and Launch Hunt are not directed to children. Launch Hunt entrants must confirm they are at least 18 years old.

Security and official identity

MADGER will never ask for a seed phrase or private key. Verify official links at madgercoin.com. The official Solana mint is BHauMX8akk2umqkQqnJwpYkCRkZmefGnEBFByeFXRKqv.

Policy changes

If app data practices materially change, this page will be updated before or when the revised app is released. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to madgercoin@gmail.com.
