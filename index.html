Sponsor, purpose, and period

The Great Burrow Meme Contest is a skill-based community creativity event sponsored by MADGER. It is not a token promotion, lottery, sweepstakes, investment offer, or purchase opportunity. It opens August 17, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. EDT and closes August 23, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. EDT. Placements will be announced by August 26, 2026 at 8:00 p.m. EDT, subject to verification.

Eligibility

Entrants must be at least 18, have reached the age of majority where they live, and be legally permitted to enter. MADGER team members, administrators, and judges cannot win. Void where participation is prohibited. A winner in a jurisdiction where receiving SOL is prohibited keeps the placement and non-monetary recognition but is ineligible for the SOL portion; that portion is not transferred or substituted.

How to enter

  1. Create an original meme featuring MADGER in his exact official likeness that you have the right to submit. New poses and scenes are welcome, but MADGER’s face, black-and-cream markings, amber-gold eyes, cream stripe, patterned tail, proportions, paws, claws, and single signature tooth must remain consistent.
  2. During the contest period, post it at t.me/madgerburrow.
  3. Include #MadgerMemeContest in the same message.
  4. Keep the message visible through the announcement.

Up to three entries per person are accepted. Images, GIFs, and short videos qualify. Posting elsewhere does not replace the Telegram submission.

Content and creator rights

Entries must be original or use authorized material. Generic badgers, altered substitute mascots, and characters with changed or missing MADGER identity features are ineligible. They must not contain wallet requests, purchase links, price or return claims, impersonation, scams, private personal information, hate, harassment, threats, sexual content, illegal activity, plagiarism, or unauthorized third-party material. Entrants retain ownership and grant MADGER a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to display, repost, crop for platform formatting, and promote the entry with reasonable creator credit in connection with the contest and community channels.

Judging and prizes

Three ineligible judges rank eligible entries using originality and comedic idea (40%), MADGER brand fit (30%), clarity and execution (20%), and community-safe humor (10%). Likes, reposts, referrals, purchases, and holdings do not count. Ties go to originality, then brand fit, then a documented judging vote. One prize per person.

First receives $20 in SOL and Burrow Meme Champion recognition; second receives $10 in SOL and Silver Shovel recognition; third receives $5 in SOL and Bronze Claw recognition. Lawful SOL amounts use Coinbase’s displayed SOL/USD spot price when MADGER initiates payment. MADGER pays network fees. Winners handle taxes, reporting, wallet security, and local legality.

Selection, safety, and payment

MADGER announces provisional placements through t.me/madgercoin and replies to each original entry. Winners must respond publicly within 72 hours. Only a SOL-eligible winner must provide a valid public Solana address. MADGER never needs a seed phrase, private key, wallet connection, signature, payment, or verification transfer. Lawful SOL prizes are sent within seven days and transaction signatures are published through an official channel.

Administration, privacy, and platforms

MADGER may pause, modify, or cancel the contest if fraud, abuse, platform failure, security risk, or circumstances outside reasonable control prevent fair administration. Public usernames, entries, and replies are used to administer, credit, moderate, and report the contest. Do not include private information.

This contest is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Telegram, X, Meta, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Google, Reddit, Discord, Solana, or Buffer. Entrants release those platforms from contest-administration responsibility to the extent allowed by law. Platform terms apply.

Questions must be posted in the official Telegram community. Trust only links published at madgercoin.com.
