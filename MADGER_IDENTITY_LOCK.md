# MADGER Absolute Identity Lock

**Authority:** Founder directive  
**Status:** Permanent, mandatory, and non-negotiable  
**Effective:** August 24, 2026

Madger's likeness must never change. Not a single identifying detail may be altered in any image, animation frame, video, sticker, poster, model, scene, or derivative.

## Locked details

- Exact face, head shape, short muzzle, nose, smile line, and silhouette.
- Exact amber-gold eye color, eye shape, pupils, highlights, placement, and expression language.
- Exact rounded cream-and-black ears.
- Exact black-and-cream facial markings.
- Exact central cream forehead stripe and its continuation across the head, neck, and back.
- Exact back stripes and every other body marking.
- Exact cream chest marking.
- Exact black fur distribution and texture language.
- Exact compact body proportions, limb lengths, stance anatomy, paws, fingers, pads, and claws.
- Exact tail shape, size, placement, and complete black-and-cream zigzag pattern.
- Exactly one signature fang/tooth, on the same canonical side and in the same canonical position shown in the approved frontal references: viewer's right / Madger's left.

## Zero-drift rule

No detail may be mirrored, shifted, resized, recolored, simplified, removed, added, approximated, redesigned, reinterpreted, or substituted. This includes details partly obscured by pose, clothing, lighting, perspective, or scenery.

New poses, expressions, actions, camera angles, lighting, clothing, accessories, and environments are allowed only when every locked identity detail remains intact.

## Required production workflow

1. Use the complete approved canonical reference pack as identity-locked input for every visual generation or edit.
2. State all locked details and the correct fang side in the production prompt.
3. Compare the result directly against the canonical front, side, back, turnaround, and expression references.
4. Inspect the face, fang side, markings, back stripes, tail pattern, proportions, paws, and claws before approval.
5. Reject the asset if even one detail differs.
6. Never publish a failed asset.
7. Never save a failed asset in an approved folder.
8. Never use a failed asset as a reference for later work.
