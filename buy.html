BEFORE YOU APPROVE ANYTHING

From wallet to swap.

  1. Prepare your wallet

    Use a Solana-compatible wallet supported by your chosen route. Install it only through the wallet provider’s official website or verified app-store listing. Keep your recovery phrase private and backed up securely. No MADGER administrator needs it.

  2. Add SOL on the Solana network

    If needed, use a provider available legally in your country and transfer SOL to your own wallet’s receive address on Solana. Confirm that it arrives. Keep some SOL available for network fees, possible token-account creation, and any priority fee; don’t spend your entire balance.

  3. Choose Raydium or Jupiter and verify everything

    Use one of the standard buttons above. Confirm the destination is exactly raydium.io or jup.ag, the input is SOL, and the output mint matches the complete MADGER address on this page. Inspect warnings rather than dismissing them. Connect your wallet only after checking the site.

    On mobile: if your regular browser cannot connect to a DEX, open the same verified route in your wallet’s built-in browser if it provides one. Never enter a recovery phrase into a website to connect.

  4. Review the quote

    Choose your own amount. Check the expected MADGER received, minimum received, price impact, route fees, and network costs. Slippage is the price movement you permit before execution—not a guaranteed fee. If the impact or settings are unacceptable, reduce the amount or stop. Do not raise slippage just to force a trade.

  5. Approve, then confirm

    If you choose to proceed, review the wallet’s transaction details and approve only the intended swap. Reject unexpected transfers or permissions. Afterward, use the transaction link to check its status and token balance changes. A pending transaction is not a confirmed purchase.

Something not working?

The wrong token appears, or MADGER is missing

Stop before signing. On your chosen DEX, select SOL as input and paste the complete MADGER mint into the output-token search. Compare the full address again. If no matching token or route is available, wait and check the official market record. Never substitute a similar ticker.

No quote, high price impact, or a failed swap

Quotes and liquidity change. Refresh the quote, check your SOL fee balance, and consider a smaller amount or waiting. Before retrying an uncertain transaction, check its status in your wallet or explorer so you don’t accidentally buy twice. Failed on-chain transactions may still cost fees. Do not bypass provider restrictions or warnings.

The transaction succeeded but my wallet doesn’t show MADGER

Refresh your wallet and inspect the confirmed transaction’s token changes. Some wallets hide unfamiliar tokens; use the full mint when checking their token-display settings. Don’t make a second purchase merely because the token is not visible yet.
