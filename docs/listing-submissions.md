# Post-launch listing and discovery submissions

Prepare evidence before launch, but submit only after public trading is authorized, the genuine pool is finalized and verified, and official channels show `TRADING_LIVE`. Eligibility, indexing, routing, timing, profile edits, and acceptance are controlled by each service and are never guaranteed. Recheck each provider's current official process and terms when submitting; avoid paid intermediaries and impersonators.

## Canonical evidence packet

Maintain one versioned packet: exact mint; Solana network; MADGER name/symbol; supply/decimals/tax; verified pool address and venue; official website/X/Telegram links; logo files and usage rights; concise non-promotional description; authority status with transaction evidence; verified controlled-wallet/allocation facts approved for disclosure; LP policy/evidence; contact email; launch UTC time; and explorer links. Two reviewers compare every address to the launch record. Never provide keys, seeds, signatures, login codes, or wallet access.

## Service checklist

| Service | Post-launch preparation and verification |
|---|---|
| DexScreener | Confirm the pair is indexed from on-chain activity; use its current profile/token-information process if eligible. Supply canonical links/artwork and verify pair/mint before sharing. Indexing or profile approval is not guaranteed. |
| Birdeye | Check token/pair discovery, network, decimals, supply, market data, and logo. Follow its current token update/verification channel with the evidence packet; never pay an unverified requester. |
| Jupiter | Confirm the pool type is supported and route discovery produces correct mint/quotes. Follow current token-list/strict-list or metadata procedures if available. Jupiter routing is not a listing promise, and liquidity does not guarantee a route. |
| CoinGecko | After genuine public trading and sufficient evidence, use the official asset-request flow. Provide verifiable market, team/contact, website/social, supply and explorer data. Its methodology, timing, and acceptance remain discretionary. |
| CoinMarketCap | Use the official request form after verifiable trading. Provide accurate project, market/pair, supply, explorer, social, and contact evidence; preserve the case ID. Do not claim pending or guaranteed listing. |
| Solana explorers | Inspect Solscan, SolanaFM, and other reputable explorers for mint, metadata, authorities, supply, decimals, holders, and pool transactions. Use only their official correction/update path and expect independent review. Explorer display is not endorsement. |

## Submission log and maintenance

Record provider, official submission URL, account owner, UTC submission/update dates, exact fields and evidence version, ticket ID, response, reviewer, and final public verification. Publish a provider claim only after independently observing it. Recheck canonical data after metadata/authority/LP changes; correct inconsistencies across the website and official channels without overstating provider status.
