1. every character of the mint and pool address;
2. supply and decimals against finalized RPC data;
3. website and social URLs against the live site;
4. authority and LP claims against transactions;
5. circulating-supply and controlled-wallet statements against reconciled balances;
6. launch time and trading status against the signed record.

Any discrepancy blocks the submission.
