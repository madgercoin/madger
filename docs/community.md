## Moderation priorities

1. Prevent loss: remove phishing, impersonation, fake mint/pool links, seed-phrase requests, wallet-drainer links, fake support, and fraudulent giveaways.
2. Preserve evidence: capture account, URL, message, UTC time, platform, and screenshots before deletion when safe.
3. Publish facts: do not speculate about price, listings, launch timing, liquidity, or incidents.
4. Keep disagreement separate from abuse: criticism is allowed; threats, targeted harassment, doxxing, hate, spam, and deception are not.
5. Escalate uncertainty: ambiguous financial, legal, security, partnership, press, or exchange messages receive no improvised answer.

## Automated-response boundary

Automation may publish only exact, pre-approved safety replies when the incoming message text and context are available and the classification is unambiguous. It must never:

- answer legal, financial, custody, liquidity, allocation, listing, partnership, incident, or security questions autonomously;
- discuss private wallets or operational controls;
- click user-supplied links or connect a wallet;
- promise timing, price, returns, volume, liquidity, or acceptance by a third party;
- continue an argument after one factual response;
- claim a message was reviewed when only aggregate comment counts were available.

Until a supported API exposes individual message text and reply actions, daily monitoring flags activity for Buffer Community/native-platform review rather than fabricating access.

## Incident procedure

For a credible scam or account compromise:

1. preserve evidence and UTC timestamps;
2. stop scheduled promotional replies if confusion could increase harm;
3. remove/report malicious content and restrict the actor;
4. verify official account, website, mint, and launch state independently;
5. publish the approved scam alert with exact facts only;
6. rotate compromised off-chain credentials and revoke stale sessions;
7. record actions, owners, platform case IDs, and resolution;
8. issue a correction if any public statement changes.

Never expose defensive details, credentials, private wallet information, or unverified accusations.
