# MADGER community operations system

## Purpose

Build a real, recognizable community around MADGER and The Burrow before public trading exists. The operating goal is meaningful repeat participation—not inflated follower counts, reply spam, or manufactured urgency.

The public state remains `MINTED_NOT_TRADING`. Community work may discuss the character, creative process, verified project facts, safety, and what members want to build. It must not imply a launch date, liquidity, venue, price outcome, partnership, or purchase path.

## Evidence loop

Run every content cycle as:

1. **Listen:** review direct messages, comments, mentions, indirect brand terms, and the current performance report.
2. **Contribute:** add useful or funny replies to relevant conversations without forcing MADGER into unrelated threads.
3. **Create:** publish one platform-native idea with one clear job—recognition, conversation, verification, or community contribution.
4. **Return:** revisit the thread, answer people by name/context, and ask one genuine follow-up when it advances the exchange.
5. **Measure:** record reach, meaningful replies, shares/saves, profile visits, community joins, and what the next test changes.

A post is not complete when it is published; it is complete after the return pass and measurement note.

Use these phrases only where they make the sentence clearer. Keyword stuffing, unrelated trending tags, and repetitive hashtag blocks reduce trust.

## Daily operating rhythm

- Review and answer genuine inbound messages first.
- Leave at most a few high-context external replies; each must add an observation, question, useful detail, or distinctive joke.
- Publish only when the idea is stronger than the current evergreen queue.
- Return to new posts after the initial conversation window and again during the next scheduled heartbeat.
- Record scams, impersonation, requests for money/access, or coordinated-activity pitches as incidents rather than engagement opportunities.

## Human response standard

Before sending, check that the response:

- acknowledges something specific the person actually said;
- varies sentence length and does not sound templated;
- avoids generic praise, sales language, and unnecessary brand insertion;
- answers the question before adding personality;
- does not pretend to have personal experiences, feelings, or off-platform knowledge;
- ends naturally—often without a call to action.

Examples are patterns, not scripts:

- **Welcome:** “Good to have you here, Maya. What pulled you into the Burrow—the character, Solana, or the chaos?”
- **Creative idea:** “That one has legs. If you had to turn it into a seven-second clip, what’s the first frame?”
- **Skeptical question:** “Fair question. The short version: the mint exists, trading isn’t public, and the site is the source of truth. Which part would you like unpacked?”
- **Promotion pitch:** “Send the audience breakdown, recent examples, disclosure terms, and the exact deliverables. We don’t use bought engagement or coordinated raids.”
- **Scam report:** “Thanks for flagging it. Don’t click or reply—send the profile URL and a screenshot with any personal information covered.”

## External conversation filter

Engage when at least one is true:

- MADGER can add a credible builder, safety, character, or community perspective;
- there is a genuine question the account can answer;
- the post invites a natural, non-promotional joke in the established voice;
- a creator’s work is relevant enough to discuss without immediately pitching them.

Skip when the only available reply is applause, a ticker drop, a forced meme, a request to follow back, or a raid-style prompt.

## Creator and KOL qualification

Before discussing collaboration, collect public evidence:

- audience geography and interests;
- median recent views, not a single peak;
- visible reply quality and suspected bot concentration;
- prior sponsorship disclosure behavior;
- examples of relevant work;
- exact deliverables, reuse rights, timing, and compensation;
- conflicts, token holdings, or incentives that require disclosure.

Never provide account access, wallet access, advance payment, exclusivity, or launch information through casual direct messages. Paid or financially material work requires explicit owner approval and written terms.

## Platform experiments

Run one-variable tests. Change only the hook, prompt, format, or posting window—not all four at once.

| Platform | First experiment | Primary signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X | concise question or useful reply with no hashtag block | substantive replies and profile visits |
| Instagram | character-led Reel with one caption prompt | shares, saves, and comments per reach |
| TikTok | 7–12 second native vertical hook with captions | completion/rewatch proxy, comments, shares |
| Facebook | community question or native Reel context | comments and shares, not raw impressions |
| YouTube Shorts | searchable title plus immediate visual hook | viewed vs. swiped away and retention |
| Telegram | one specific discussion prompt with active follow-up | distinct participants and returning participants |

Use `reports/social/latest.md` for the automated Buffer view and native analytics when the connector omits retention, profile visits, or comment content. Do not infer unavailable values as zero.

## Escalation and stop conditions

Stop and preserve context when a conversation involves:

- requests for funds, wallets, seed phrases, private keys, or account access;
- legal commitments, compensation, exclusivity, partnership claims, or launch details;
- harassment, doxxing, threats, or credible impersonation;
- coordinated buying, artificial engagement, spam raids, or deceptive promotion;
- a factual conflict involving the official mint or public launch state.

Public corrections must use verified facts and official channels. Delete or hide content only when platform safety or moderation policy clearly supports it; otherwise preserve evidence and escalate.
