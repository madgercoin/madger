## Analytical approach

The accounts are new and do not yet have enough platform-specific history for statistically reliable “best time” conclusions. This calendar therefore uses structured exploration rather than generic averages:

- test morning, afternoon, evening, and late-evening windows;
- separate identical creative across platforms to reduce simultaneous audience cannibalization;
- use one primary objective per post;
- preserve comparable hooks so performance differences are interpretable;
- evaluate after Buffer’s metrics have refreshed, normally up to roughly 24 hours later;
- promote a time slot only after at least three comparable observations.

Primary metrics: 3-second/video views where available, completion proxy, reactions, comments, shares/reposts, saves, profile visits/follows, and engagement rate. Missing metrics are not treated as zero.
