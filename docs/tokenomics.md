## Launch controls still requiring approval

No launch transaction should proceed until the signed launch record specifies:

1. venue and pool design;
2. liquidity budget and SOL/fee reserve;
3. opening valuation and allowed setup tolerance;
4. LP custody and lock, burn, or retention policy;
5. exact launch UTC time and delay window;
6. mint, freeze, and metadata authority status and change timing;
7. allocation-transfer schedule and destination wallets;
8. proposer, verifier, approvers, emergency owners, and alternates.

## Transparency standard

- Publish only finalized, independently verified facts.
- Identify controlled wallets and vesting, custody, or transfer restrictions when approved for disclosure.
- Link LP and authority claims to on-chain evidence; never rely on a badge or screenshot.
- Reconcile every controlled-wallet movement against the approved allocation schedule.
- Do not promise price, liquidity, exchange listings, returns, or appreciation.
- Correct inconsistencies across the website, explorers, listings, and social channels promptly.

## Change control

Any change to supply, allocations, authority policy, liquidity policy, or controlled-wallet disclosures requires a versioned decision record with the approver, UTC timestamp, rationale, and supporting on-chain or legal review. Signing material never belongs in this repository.
