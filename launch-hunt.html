Official Rules — MADGER Launch Hunt
  1. No purchase necessary. A purchase, token holding, trade, wallet balance, payment, or other financial activity is not required and will not increase the chance of winning.
  2. Sponsor. The promotion is administered by MADGER through the official website madgercoin.com. Promotion questions may be sent to madgercoin@gmail.com.
  3. Promotion period. The promotion begins when the official MADGER account publishes the Launch Hunt announcement and ends August 28, 2026 at 14:00 UTC. Sponsor's received-email timestamp controls for entry timing.
  4. Eligibility. Open to natural persons age 18 or older who may lawfully participate and receive the stated prize where they live. Void where prohibited or restricted. MADGER administrators, persons directly administering the drawing, and their immediate household members are not eligible.
  5. Core entry. During the promotion period, find the Burrow Field Mark on madgercoin.com, remain a member of the official MADGER Telegram community at the time of drawing, and email the required entry information to madgercoin@gmail.com using the subject “MADGER Launch Hunt Entry.” A correct field mark and verifiable Telegram username are required.
  6. Bonus entry. An eligible entrant may receive one additional entry by publishing one original public X post during the promotion period that mentions @madgercoin, includes #MADGERGiveaway, and is accessible for verification when entries are reviewed. The post may express any genuine view; positive sentiment is not required. Include the post URL in the entry email.
  7. Entry limit. Maximum two entry units per person: one core entry and one optional X bonus entry. Multiple accounts, identities, email addresses, repeated posts, automation, copied or near-duplicate submissions, purchased engagement, or other attempts to circumvent the limit may result in disqualification.
  8. Prizes. Six prizes totaling $75 USDC: one $30 USDC prize, one $20 USDC prize, one $10 USDC prize, and three $5 USDC prizes. One prize maximum per person. Prize transfer will be made on Solana after winner verification. A wallet is not required to enter.
  9. Drawing and odds. Winners will be selected by random drawing from eligible entry units after the promotion closes. A selected person is removed from subsequent prize draws. Odds depend on the number of eligible entry units received. Sponsor may verify eligibility, Telegram membership, X bonus posts, and compliance before awarding a prize.
  10. Winner contact. Potential winners will be contacted using the email address used to enter. A potential winner must respond within 72 hours and may be required to confirm eligibility and provide a Solana wallet address for prize delivery. Failure to respond or inability to lawfully receive the prize may result in selection of an alternate winner.
  11. Taxes and fees. Winners are responsible for any taxes, reporting obligations, wallet costs, or other obligations that apply to them. MADGER will not ask a winner to pay MADGER a fee to receive a prize.
  12. Integrity. Sponsor may disqualify entries reasonably determined to involve fraud, impersonation, automation, manipulation, multiple-person identities, or material violation of these rules. Sponsor will not arbitrarily reject an otherwise eligible entry.
  13. Platform independence. This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with X, Telegram, Solana, Circle, or Raydium. Entrants release those platforms and entities from responsibility for administering this promotion to the extent permitted by law.
  14. Privacy. Entry information is used to administer the promotion, prevent fraud, contact potential winners, document prize fulfillment, and comply with applicable obligations. Do not include sensitive personal information, seed phrases, private keys, or wallet credentials in an entry.
  15. Launch independence. The giveaway is separate from any MADGER token launch or market activity. Launch timing may change or be cancelled. Participation creates no right to receive tokens, purchase tokens, or access a market.
  16. General. Sponsor may suspend, cancel, or modify the promotion if fraud, technical failure, legal restriction, security incident, or another condition materially impairs fair administration, provided that already-earned legitimate rights are handled as fairly as reasonably possible. These rules are subject to applicable law.
