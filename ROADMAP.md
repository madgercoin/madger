# MADGER roadmap

Roadmap items are directions, not promises, dates, investment expectations, or proof of completion.

## Phase 1 — Build The Burrow (in progress)

Maintain the approved brand, production website, litepaper, official mint verification, safety language, community channels, original content, contributor standards, and the reviewed launch control package. The centrally controlled state remains `MINTED_NOT_TRADING`; readiness documentation does not imply trading availability or authorize a transaction.

## Phase 2 — Public launch readiness

Resolve the founder approvals in `LAUNCH_DECISIONS.md`, pass the runbook go/no-go gates, and—if and when authorized—disclose final verified method/timing, authority and liquidity facts, material risks, and official-channel verification. No date, liquidity pool, listing, venue, or availability is currently announced.

## Phase 3 — Sustainable community growth

Evaluate education, recurring original content, moderation, community campaigns, credible listings/partnerships only after they exist, and maintainable operations. Growth must not depend on manufactured hype.

## Phase 4 — Ecosystem utility

Research brand extensions and practical cryptocurrency payments for ordinary activities. Any wallet, merchant, or payment system requires demonstrated user need, legal/compliance review, accessibility, privacy, custody boundaries, threat modeling, support, monitoring, and sustainable ownership before implementation.

## Future Decisions

Dates, phase exit criteria, budget, owners, governance, launch structure, token economics, partners, listings, payment markets/providers/assets, and measurable outcomes remain undecided.
