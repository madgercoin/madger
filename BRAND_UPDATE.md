# Final branding update

This update replaces the earlier mixed MADGER concepts with the final approved identity.

## Approved

- Cute, trustworthy, permanently unimpressed 3D Honey Badger
- Black-and-white fur, cream details, white lightning/zigzag forehead stripe
- Large round eyes, softer eyebrows, puffier cheeks, round ears
- Short thick arms, reduced claws, crossed-arms full-character pose, tiny fang
- Black, charcoal, bone, cream, sand, bronze, and gold palette
- Gold-edged circular emblem and distressed brush MADGER wordmark
- “Small Badger. Big Mood.”
- “Stay Mad.”
- “Keep Digging.”
- “The Burrow Never Sleeps.”
- “The Badger. The Attitude. The Coin.”

## Retired

- Green-accented identity and leaf-heavy layouts
- “We don’t follow. We lead the Burrow.”
- Cuban-chain/thug mascot
- Sunglasses, clothing, chains, medallions, and open-arms pose

All public raster assets and contributor-facing brand documentation must follow the approved identity above.
