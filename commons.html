THE COMMUNITY COMPACT

SUPPORT WITH
GUARDRAILS.

A community becomes useful when people know what is expected, what is protected, and which claims nobody is authorized to make.

ACCESS

Support is not a holder privilege

No purchase is required to use public resources, report a problem, join the community, or propose work. $MADGER is the shared symbol of the network—not a ticket to basic help.

RECOGNITION

Credit useful work visibly

Accepted contributions may be credited by the creator's preferred public handle and considered for a community spotlight. Publication and payment are never implied until confirmed.

VISUAL AUTHORSHIP

Human direction is mandatory

Every official visual must preserve the exact approved likeness, serve a clear communication purpose, and pass human review. AI-assisted work gets no exemption from craft, originality, disclosure rules, or accountability.

SAFETY

No secrets. No pressure. No promises.

No legitimate MADGER contributor needs a seed phrase, private key, password, remote wallet access, verification payment, or promise that the token will appreciate.
