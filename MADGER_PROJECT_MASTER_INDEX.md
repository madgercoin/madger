## 3. Non-negotiable Madger character canon

This section overrides any looser creative prompt or generated variation.

### 3.0 Absolute identity lock

**Madger's likeness is immutable. Not a single identifying detail may ever change.** Every future image, animation frame, video, sticker, poster, model, or derivative must use the approved canonical reference pack as identity-locked input. This includes the exact fang/tooth side and placement, face and muzzle geometry, amber-gold eyes, ears, every black-and-cream facial and body marking, the complete forehead-to-back stripe pattern, all back stripes, chest marking, tail shape and full tail pattern, body proportions, limbs, paws, claws, fur distribution, and silhouette.

New poses, expressions, lighting, camera angles, accessories, and environments are permitted only when every canonical identity detail remains unchanged. A result with even one altered, missing, mirrored, added, shifted, recolored, resized, or reinterpreted detail is a failed asset and must be rejected. It may never be published, archived as approved, or used as a future reference.

- Cute, confident, premium 3D honey-badger character.
- Exact established face, muzzle, proportions, paws/claws, and expression language.
- Black-and-cream markings with a cream stripe running from forehead into the body.
- Amber-gold expressive eyes.
- Cream rounded ears.
- Patterned black-and-cream tail.
- **Exactly one permanent signature tooth/fang on its fixed canonical side.** Never zero teeth, two teeth, or a tooth moved to the opposite side.
- Exact canonical forehead, head, neck, and back stripe arrangement; never simplify, mirror, shift, add, or remove any stripe or marking.
