Edition 3.1 adds thirteen illustrated landmark interiors, local histories, 221 registered furnishings, thirteen measured furnished plans and two complete companion short stories. It extends the 3.0 world foundation below; it does not replace its geography or character cards.

The current foundation develops eight regions, fourteen principal communities, forty named characters, nine factions and twelve creatures. Four season outlines and twelve standalone stories connect personal choices to local history and competing loyalties.

## Fixed Burrow foundation

The original sixteen surface area positions and footprints, three canal crossings, watercourse and surface route graph remain unchanged. Twenty-one underground areas use the same coordinate system, with occupied levels at -12 and -24 m and services at -36 m. The planning baseline remains 800 surface and 1,600 underground residents. Public daytime access and inner protective closure are distinct states.

## Story and production continuity

Episode one stays inside the Burrow, beginning with joyful ordinary life before subtle clues appear. Writer-only truth must not become premature audience exposition. Preserve all four established heroes and approved voices. Snow White uses canine sounds only; Bot has a mechanical face; Madger has no oversized fang or decorative tear.

Edition 3.0 resolves previously unassigned exterior geography and companion origins. It preserves the revised Madger founding history, living parents, collective construction, estranged Nix relationship and hidden rescue funding. Read the [character index](characters/README.md) before writing a new appearance.

Assign each scene recorded character and location IDs. Measured geometry controls placement; visual references control appearance. Track changes to buildings, possessions, relationships and knowledge by story date. Preserve old editions and explain any later canon replacement explicitly.

This archive contains fictional production plans and developed story outlines. It does not claim a finished animated season, game implementation or construction engineering design. Existing game courses retain their recorded layouts until a versioned connection is authored.
