# MADGER Factions
Development edition 3.0.0

## FAC-01 - Burrow Commons

**Alignment**: Good / civic

**Home**: The Burrow

**Purpose**: Keep a welcoming town livable, fair and defensible. This names the civic cooperation already present in the Bible; it does not replace ward decisions with a new ruler.

**Leadership**: Ward delegates, open meetings and rotating work coordinators. Madger convenes and serves; his founder status gives him no permanent veto. Snow is a protected companion, not an office-holder.

**Methods**: Shared stores, maintenance rosters, public explanations, rescue practice and accountable protective closures. Disputes concern priorities and evidence, not whether ordinary residents deserve safety.

**Tension**: Welcome versus capacity. Pell wants understandable procedures; Iona wants verified limits; Madger sometimes promises both before consulting either.

**Ties**: Commonworks supplies expertise; the Wayfarer Accord brings visitors and goods. The Commons opposes coercion by the Gilt Ledger, intrusion by Glass Thread and predation by Riven Claw.

## FAC-02 - Commonworks Fellowship

**Alignment**: Good / mutual aid

**Home**: Stonewake and a regional workshop network

**Purpose**: Make essential knowledge teachable so no settlement depends on one indispensable specialist.

**Leadership**: Oren Vale coordinates exchanges. Iona is the Burrow contact; Tavi is an apprentice participant, never an unsupervised emergency specialist.

**Methods**: Travelling repair crews, apprenticeships, shared specifications and tool loans. Each town keeps local authority over its own machinery.

**Tension**: Their careful standards can postpone urgent improvisation. Oren must learn when a temporary repair is responsible, while Madger must accept when it is unsafe.

**Ties**: Works with the Commons and Wayfarers. Refuses exclusive maintenance deals from the Gilt Ledger. Glass Thread can steal records, but cannot acquire practical mastery by downloading them.

## FAC-03 - Wayfarer Accord

**Alignment**: Good / travel and refuge

**Home**: Latchford; independent crews across the road network

**Purpose**: Keep travel, rescue and honest exchange possible for people without powerful sponsors.

**Leadership**: Sela Venn coordinates convoy safety. Lark Quill carries dispatches and keeps route accounts. Crews can decline a route they cannot safely serve.

**Methods**: Guides, escorted caravans, weather reports and voluntary rescue agreements. Their wagons are finite; an invitation does not create transport capacity.

**Tension**: Keeping a promised timetable may conflict with stopping for strangers. Sela supports Madger but will not endorse a comforting promise she cannot fulfill.

**Ties**: Supplies the Burrow and transports Commonworks crews. Pays legitimate tolls, contests Gilt Ledger coercion, and resists Riven Claw raids. Lark occasionally buys information from Pip Skein.

## FAC-04 - Gilt Ledger

**Alignment**: Hostile / coercive commerce

**Home**: Privately controlled warehouses and offices in Reedbank

**Purpose**: Make independent settlements reliant on its depots, credit and permission to trade. Its public promise is dependable supply; its private objective is lasting leverage.

**Leadership**: Director Veyra Kest sets policy. Corvin Silt brokers contracts and influence. Bale Nacre controls stock, transport allocations and collections.

**Methods**: Exclusive supply terms, selective shortages, pressure on indebted operators and purchased information. It owns particular businesses, not Reedbank or every merchant.

**Tension**: Veyra calls dependence stability. Corvin profits from ambiguity; Bale knows that a frightened workforce eventually stops cooperating.

**Ties**: Buys limited information jobs from Glass Thread through Corvin. It has no command over the hacker crew. It sometimes buys stolen goods through intermediaries but regards Riven Claw as an expensive disruption.

## FAC-05 - Glass Thread

**Alignment**: Hostile / information crime

**Home**: Changing rented workrooms around Latchford

**Purpose**: Sell privileged information and turn uncertain records into influence. Members claim that exposing weak systems excuses exploiting the people who rely on them.

**Leadership**: Nix Vey chooses targets and negotiates jobs. Tessera Keen handles social access and forged identities. Patch Serein maintains stolen equipment and relays.

**Methods**: Fictional record tampering, impersonation and constrained system intrusions. Access requires a particular opportunity; local controls, human checks and physical barriers still matter.

**Tension**: Nix wants recognition; Tessera wants profitable anonymity; Patch wants an exit before someone is physically harmed. Remorse does not erase responsibility.

**Ties**: Accepts selected Gilt Ledger contracts and keeps its own secrets. Distrusts Riven Claw, whose violence destroys useful contacts. Writer-only v1.1 answer: Tessera’s limited relay probe causes D17. Madger does not know Glass Thread’s membership at the pilot; Snow’s scent is unrelated.

## FAC-06 - Riven Claw

**Alignment**: Hostile / raiding band

**Home**: Disused freight terraces in the Cinder Cuts

**Purpose**: Extract supplies and obedience from travellers and weaker outposts. Its leader treats possession as something proved through force.

**Leadership**: Brakka Mourn commands raids. Dreg Husk controls hauling and shares. Vessa Rake scouts routes and bargains for intelligence.

**Methods**: Ambush, intimidation and theft of movable supplies. They need food, rest, transport and local knowledge; they cannot appear anywhere without a route.

**Tension**: Brakka prizes spectacle, Dreg wants sustainable provisions, and Vessa resents being treated as expendable. Their cooperation depends on rewards and fear.

**Ties**: Sells through fences including Pip, who can refuse a deal. It has occasional transactions with Gilt Ledger intermediaries, no standing alliance. No species is inherently loyal to this band.

## FAC-07 - Free Harbors Compact

**Alignment**: Allied / independent communities

**Home**: A treaty among Crownrest, Ossara public wells, Nacre Quay, Aurel Quay and Lumen Tide; no capital

**Purpose**: Protect passage, rescue rights and local ownership of knowledge without turning mutual aid into annexation.

**Leadership**: Rotating local delegates; any new obligation requires the affected community’s consent.

**Methods**: Reciprocal refuge, shared weather observations and narrowly scoped archive exchanges.

**Tension**: Secrecy protects some communities but can endanger visiting travelers. Wealthier ports try to dominate timetables.

**Ties**: Works with Wayfarers and Commonworks. Opposes water coercion, sky ransom and extraction without agreement.

## FAC-08 - Blackwake Sails

**Alignment**: Hostile / sky extortion

**Home**: A mobile fleet using an abandoned mooring outside Aurel Quay

**Purpose**: Turn emergency passage into permanent tribute.

**Leadership**: Serik commands three small cargo craft; captains bargain over shares and can desert.

**Methods**: False promises, seized cargo and ransom; real rescue skill makes the deception credible.

**Tension**: Crew members want profit and survival, not Serik’s theater of heroism.

**Ties**: Occasional trade with Riven Claw; no global villain command structure. Eri knows their procedures.

## FAC-09 - Still Bell Archive

**Alignment**: Divided institution / protective and authoritarian wings

**Home**: Hushvale Echo Archive

**Purpose**: Preserve Severance evidence and assess dangerous resonant crossings.

**Leadership**: Ossa controls the senior council; Ysolde leads a disclosure faction.

**Methods**: Technical records, measured closure orders and field inspections. Ossa additionally forged a modern prohibition.

**Tension**: Real expertise can become a monopoly on judgment. Most archivists are neither villains nor infallible sages.

**Ties**: Needs independent Hollow Sea records; disputed by Free Harbors; Commonworks can test physical claims.
