# MADGER Communities
Development edition 3.0.0

## BRW - The Burrow

**Region**: REG-01

**Layout**: basin

**Population**: 2400

**Appearance**: A welcoming limestone basin and a larger inhabited underground town.

**Government**: Ward assemblies and shared services; Madger has no permanent veto.

**Economy**: Food gardens, workshops, care, teaching and caravan exchange. Imports salt, metals, medicine and some grain.

**Custom**: Bridge Day celebrates ordinary repairs with races, food and deliberately terrible songs. Surface and underground households exchange supper invitations.

**Safe**: Sunwell and Underhall by day; inner shelter boundary during a surface overrun.

**Danger**: No new exterior route, shaft or bypass is added to its sealed defenses.

**History**: Six years of collective construction grew from a camp and common store into five neighborhoods. Existing footprints and route graphs are inherited verbatim.

**Conflict**: Welcome without dependence; the missing-road crisis tests whether fear will make the town surrender its values.

**Buildings**: Existing Burrow plans control all 37 primary areas and seven detailed rooms.

**Datum Z M**: 0

## EXT-01 - Reedbank

**Region**: REG-01

**Layout**: river

**Population**: 3600

**Appearance**: Raised river houses, terracotta roofs, timber wharves and long flood stairs.

**Government**: An elected harbor council; depot owners can petition but do not own the river.

**Economy**: Boat repair, reeds, ceramics and fish; grain arrives from the floodplain.

**Custom**: At Watermark Night families repaint their flood marks and launch harmless paper boats carrying promises they actually kept.

**Safe**: High-bank kitchen and public lofts during flood season.

**Danger**: Low wharves close in floodwater; boats follow marked channels.

**History**: Founded 240 years ago around a ferry; survived the Severance on local river trade. The flood seven years ago scattered households and strengthened depot power.

**Conflict**: Dara and Hollis remain here. Veyra buys influence while kitchen volunteers investigate unfair allotments.

**Buildings**: Reedbank Kitchen|Flood Charter House|Kest Depot

**Datum Z M**: 4

## EXT-02 - Stonewake

**Region**: REG-01

**Layout**: quarry

**Population**: 2160

**Appearance**: Occupied quarry terraces, stone arches, copper roof caps and bright workshop shutters.

**Government**: Workshop delegates, quarry residents and water crews share a three-chamber council.

**Economy**: Cut stone, metal fittings and trained builders; imports grain and timber.

**Custom**: The Unfinished Fair exhibits useful failures alongside fine craft; apprentices explain what they learned.

**Safe**: Public lower yard and braced civic galleries.

**Danger**: Old quarry faces have marked collapse zones; deep descent is licensed and physically separate from the Burrow.

**History**: Founded 310 years ago; abandoned extraction faces were converted into homes. A work-platform failure nine years ago changed local training rules.

**Conflict**: Eris wants productive change; Oren fears rushing. Gorilla must return as a colleague rather than the one who must save everyone.

**Buildings**: Commonworks Hall|White Family Workshop|Deep Descent House

**Datum Z M**: 320

## EXT-03 - Latchford

**Region**: REG-01

**Layout**: crossroads

**Population**: 2880

**Appearance**: A bustling junction town of inns, arcades, wagon courts and public dispatch boards.

**Government**: Rotating neighborhood council and an independent route tribunal.

**Economy**: Transport, repair, lodging and records; reliable information is an export.

**Custom**: The Six Roads Supper seats strangers by destination, not rank. Stories are traded before prices.

**Safe**: Accord courtyard and staffed arrival houses.

**Danger**: An unfamiliar seal is checked against a second source; private rented rooms are not automatic criminal sites.

**History**: Grew around three roads 180 years ago. The Severance made it a transfer hub; relay trade arrived 38 years ago.

**Conflict**: Glass Thread rents limited rooms here. Honest dispatchers resent outsiders treating the whole town as a den of criminals.

**Buildings**: Accord Convoy House|Six Roads Inn|North Relay Rooms

**Datum Z M**: 12

## EXT-04 - Millmere

**Region**: REG-01

**Layout**: canal

**Population**: 3600

**Appearance**: Working canals, painted mill wheels, brick workshops and narrow planted bridges.

**Government**: Canal wards elect stewards; worker cooperatives operate many workshops.

**Economy**: Paper, instruments, small machines and food milling; imports ore and hardwood.

**Custom**: During Wheelstill Day every mill stops for inspection, music and races using hand-powered toy boats.

**Safe**: Canal schools and elevated public bridges.

**Danger**: Wheel pits and locked sluice works are industrial hazards, not secret monster entrances.

**History**: A mill settlement for 220 years. Unequal restoration after a flood pushed some young residents away; Nix learned to distrust official accounts.

**Conflict**: Lume wants Bot to visit without feeling summoned. Nix tries to turn old neglect into permission to injure new strangers.

**Buildings**: Varr Workshop|Canal Records House|Wheelstill Market

**Datum Z M**: 8

## EXT-05 - Willowmere

**Region**: REG-01

**Layout**: orchard

**Population**: 1728

**Appearance**: Orchard lanes, rain gardens, low timber teaching halls and the first enormous canopy roots.

**Government**: Growing circles and household delegates; water decisions are recorded publicly.

**Economy**: Fruit, seeds, dyes and local guides; imports tools and salt.

**Custom**: Seed Exchange pairs every rare seed with the story of who kept it alive. Children invent impossible plants in clay.

**Safe**: Teaching courtyards and the fixed ridge path.

**Danger**: Migration-season root corridors are closed at dusk until guides inspect them.

**History**: Founded 145 years ago by growers and guides at the forest margin. It stayed small by choice rather than isolation.

**Conflict**: Neri and Sori disagree about returning; each has relatives and responsibilities in two places.

**Buildings**: Seed House|Ridge Teaching Hall|Canopy Guide Lodge

**Datum Z M**: 72

## EXT-06 - Cinder Cuts

**Region**: REG-07

**Layout**: freight

**Population**: 1152

**Appearance**: Old freight cuttings, exposed basalt terraces, repaired worker homes and abandoned sidings.

**Government**: Three occupied terraces maintain a residents council; Riven Claw occupies only the disused eastern spur.

**Economy**: Salvage, hauling and ceramic repair; independent workers trade with Kettle Crown.

**Custom**: The Last Whistle fair honors retired engines with percussion and shared food.

**Safe**: Occupied west terraces and public worker hostel.

**Danger**: Eastern Spur is controlled by Riven Claw; steam trenches and unstable freight edges are marked.

**History**: Built 75 years ago as a freight district after the Severance. A route diversion 18 years ago reduced work; raiders arrived seven years ago.

**Conflict**: Dreg has family in the legitimate neighborhood. Brakka tries to turn neglected workers into a captive supply base.

**Buildings**: Workers Hostel|Salvage Exchange|Eastern Spur Stronghold

**Datum Z M**: 410

## EXT-07 - Crownrest

**Region**: REG-02

**Layout**: canopy

**Population**: 1440

**Appearance**: A settlement on six colossal living crowns, joined by adjustable bridges above a fixed stone ridge.

**Government**: Each crown chooses a delegate; bridge crews can suspend a crossing despite a festival timetable.

**Economy**: Resin craft, living fiber, medicine plants and route guidance; grains are stored in the fixed ridge depot.

**Custom**: The Returning Shade festival reunites crowns after migration; dancers adjust tempo to swaying branches.

**Safe**: Fixed Ridge House during storms; tethered crown decks in normal weather.

**Danger**: A drifting crown changes its local position by up to 180 m during wet season; the baseline plan records spring anchor positions.

**History**: Founded 160 years ago by orchard people and canopy guides. Its architecture moves with trees rather than forcing roots to stand still.

**Conflict**: Senn knows a route that records call abandoned. Saving outsiders may expose a shelter his community promised to keep quiet.

**Buildings**: Ridge House|Bridgewright Pavilion|Resin Exchange

**Datum Z M**: 180

## EXT-08 - Ossara

**Region**: REG-03

**Layout**: rings

**Population**: 5760

**Appearance**: A sandstone city of shaded courts, wind towers, turquoise shutters and glass dunes beyond its walls.

**Government**: Public well assemblies and craft wards; the Salt Crown company is a powerful rival, not the government.

**Economy**: Optical glass, salt, astronomy and caravan goods; roof cisterns and three seasonal aquifers support agriculture.

**Custom**: The Night of Two Shadows uses lanterns and mirrored sails for street theater; guests must bring a story, not a gift.

**Safe**: Public cistern courts and deep caravan inns.

**Danger**: Dune travel stops during glass storms; reflected light is not a reliable navigation bearing.

**History**: Founded 420 years ago at three wells. An old observatory survived the Severance, but its archives preserve only fragments.

**Conflict**: Maela discovers some water debts are fabricated. Kavar sells stability while deliberately keeping a competing well closed.

**Buildings**: Three Wells Court|Mirror Observatory|Salt Crown Counting House

**Datum Z M**: 95

## EXT-09 - Nacre Quay

**Region**: REG-04

**Layout**: tidal

**Population**: 4320

**Appearance**: A coral-colored port wrapped around stepped tidal basins and limestone arcades.

**Government**: Harbor crews, salvage families and reef tenders elect a tide council.

**Economy**: Sailing, salt fish, reef gardening and salvage; fresh water comes from roof collection and inland springs.

**Custom**: Low Tide Theater stages plays on steps exposed for only an hour; everyone helps strike the stage before water returns.

**Safe**: Upper quay, rescue house and marked moorings.

**Danger**: The drowned archive opens only around low tide; reefback migration closes the outer channel seasonally.

**History**: Founded 280 years ago above an older drowned district. The sinking preceded the Severance; neither event caused the other.

**Conflict**: Toma must choose between a profitable salvage claim and evidence belonging to living descendant communities.

**Buildings**: Tide Council Hall|Rescue Sail House|Drowned Archive

**Datum Z M**: 0

## EXT-10 - Aurel Quay

**Region**: REG-05

**Layout**: sky

**Population**: 2304

**Appearance**: Three floating basalt islands joined by cargo spans, with counterweights, hanging gardens and ochre sails.

**Government**: Independent dock cooperatives and neighborhood crews; no captain owns an island.

**Economy**: Weather routing, liftstone shaping and sail making; food arrives by cargo craft and terrace gardens.

**Custom**: Weight Day is a comedy festival where teams build the lightest absurd object that still performs its job.

**Safe**: Sheltered inner moorings and grounded storm refuges on each island.

**Danger**: Liftstone responds to local fields, with strict load budgets; it cannot be carried elsewhere to float arbitrary cities.

**History**: Established 130 years ago around seasonal anchor fields. Its first charter outlawed selling residents with a vessel.

**Conflict**: Eri refuses a lucrative overloaded launch. Serik offers stranded crews a rescue that becomes a protection racket.

**Buildings**: Counterweight Hall|Sailmakers Court|Windward Rescue Dock

**Datum Z M**: 1600

## EXT-11 - Hushvale

**Region**: REG-06

**Layout**: cavern

**Population**: 2016

**Appearance**: A geothermal town under a glacier shoulder, with copper roof caps, steam gardens and blue ice light.

**Government**: Winter guilds and household delegates share stores; the archive order holds expertise, not unlimited civil authority.

**Economy**: Insulation cloth, cold storage, optical ice study and mountain guiding.

**Custom**: The Quiet Hour marks storms survived; the following feast is deliberately noisy and full of imitation bird calls.

**Safe**: Warm civic cavern and staffed wayhouses.

**Danger**: Echo ice can repeat old sounds; choirwraith habitat is mapped beyond the warm ridge.

**History**: Founded 360 years ago around springs. Routekeepers here cut resonance links during the Severance and inherited both gratitude and blame.

**Conflict**: Ysolde finds a true warning mixed with a forged prohibition. Ossa cannot accept that stewardship requires other people to judge the evidence.

**Buildings**: Winter Hall|Echo Archive|Warm Ridge Observatory

**Datum Z M**: 980

## EXT-12 - Kettle Crown

**Region**: REG-07

**Layout**: volcanic

**Population**: 3456

**Appearance**: A city on volcanic shelves, with obsidian stairs, red ceramic roofs and lush hot-spring gardens.

**Government**: Forge cooperatives, growers and freight neighborhoods negotiate heat and water shares.

**Economy**: Ceramics, tempered metal and heat-grown fruit; imports timber and cool-water crops.

**Custom**: The Cooling Feast celebrates a finished kiln cycle; rival makers exchange deliberately ridiculous cups.

**Safe**: Stone civic courts and supervised hot-spring gardens.

**Danger**: Gas troughs and active channels close under marked conditions; lava is not casually crossed by being brave.

**History**: Founded 190 years ago on old cooled shelves. Industrial growth enriched the city while the diverted freight line harmed Cinder Cuts.

**Conflict**: Kesh must acknowledge the town profited from a decision that hurt its neighbors without accepting Brakka as their representative.

**Buildings**: Common Kiln Hall|Spring Garden House|Freight Mediation Hall

**Datum Z M**: 620

## EXT-13 - Lumen Tide

**Region**: REG-08

**Layout**: deepsea

**Population**: 4608

**Appearance**: A vertical limestone city above a subterranean ocean, with mineral windows and fungal shore gardens.

**Government**: Harbor, air and food circles elect rotating speakers; surface visitors follow local law.

**Economy**: Fungal textiles, mineral instruments and cavern navigation; imports grains through Stonewake trade.

**Custom**: The Lantern Descent lowers lamps to remember people who left on voyages; it ends with dancing, not a funeral.

**Safe**: Ventilated upper civic terraces and inspected refuge docks.

**Danger**: Pressure, air quality and sea level determine access; no passage connects it directly to the Burrow.

**History**: A continuous community for at least 600 years, with several written founding traditions. Its oldest harbor predates the Open Ways.

**Conflict**: Namu wants surface partnership on equal terms. A rediscovered chart threatens to invite extractive expeditions into sacred nesting waters.

**Buildings**: Tide Speakers Hall|Fungal Exchange|Pressure Gate House

**Datum Z M**: -1600
